# Website Copywriting Best Practices

## Core Principles

### Clarity Over Cleverness

- The hero section must immediately communicate: Who you are, What you do, How it helps the client
- People scan web pages - headlines should convey complete understanding
- No one should wonder what the company does after reading the hero

### Scannability

- Use headlines to do the heavy lifting
- Body copy supports and expands on headlines
- Keep paragraphs short (2-4 lines maximum)
- Use white space generously
- Bullet points for lists, but sparingly

### Conciseness

- Say things as concisely as possible to get the point across
- Long enough to make impact, short enough not to overwhelm
- Cut unnecessary words and phrases
- One idea per paragraph

## Page-Specific Guidelines

### Homepage

**Hero Section (Above the Fold):**

- Clear headline: What you do + who it's for
- Supporting subheadline: The benefit or transformation
- Primary CTA button
- Optional: Supporting visual or trust indicator
- Goal: Instant clarity on what this company does

**Structure:**

1. Hero - Immediate clarity
2. Problem/Pain Point - Show you understand their struggle
3. Solution Overview - How you solve it (3-5 key benefits)
4. How It Works - Simple 3-4 step process
5. Social Proof - Testimonials, logos, results
6. Final CTA - Clear next step

### About Page

**Focus:**

- Why you exist (mission/purpose)
- Who you serve (ICP)
- What makes you different
- The team's expertise
- Your values and approach

**Tone:** More personal, story-driven, Level 4 (Empowerment) messaging

### Service Pages

**Structure:**

1. Service headline + benefit
2. Who this is for
3. What's included
4. How it works / Process
5. Results/outcomes
6. Social proof specific to this service
7. CTA

**Focus:** Specific, tactical, benefit-driven

### Contact Page

Keep it simple:

- Clear headline about next steps
- Brief description of what happens after they reach out
- Form fields (minimal - only what you need)
- Alternative contact methods
- Response time expectations

## Industry-Specific Considerations

### Professional Services (Consulting, Legal, Financial)

**Tone:** Professional, authoritative, trustworthy
**Focus:** Expertise, credentials, results, process clarity
**Language:** Industry-appropriate but accessible
**CTAs:** "Schedule Consultation," "Discuss Your Needs," "Get Expert Guidance"

**Key Elements:**

- Credentials and certifications
- Case studies with specific outcomes
- Clear process/methodology
- Trust indicators (years in business, clients served)

### SaaS (Software as a Service)

**Tone:** Modern, efficient, innovative
**Focus:** Problem → Solution → Results speed
**Language:** Benefit-driven, technical accuracy without jargon
**CTAs:** "Start Free Trial," "See Demo," "Get Started," "Book Demo"

**Key Elements:**

- Clear feature → benefit translation
- Integration capabilities
- Scalability messaging
- Security/compliance if relevant
- Free trial or demo offer

### Local Services (Home services, healthcare, etc.)

**Tone:** Friendly, approachable, community-focused
**Focus:** Local expertise, convenience, trust
**Language:** Conversational, practical
**CTAs:** "Call Now," "Book Appointment," "Get Free Quote"

**Key Elements:**

- Service area clearly stated
- Local reviews/testimonials
- Response time commitments
- Licensing/insurance information
- Before/after examples

## CTA Best Practices

### Button Copy Guidelines

**Action-Oriented:**

- Start with verbs: "Get," "Start," "Schedule," "Download," "Book"
- Be specific: "Get Your Free Audit" not "Submit"
- Create urgency when appropriate: "Start Your Free Trial Today"

**By Buyer Journey Stage:**

**Awareness Stage (Top of funnel):**

- "Learn More"
- "Download Guide"
- "Get Free Resource"
- "Watch Demo"

**Consideration Stage (Middle of funnel):**

- "See How It Works"
- "Compare Solutions"
- "Schedule Consultation"
- "Request Quote"

**Decision Stage (Bottom of funnel):**

- "Get Started"
- "Start Free Trial"
- "Buy Now"
- "Schedule Onboarding Call"

## SEO Integration

### On-Page Elements

**H1 (One per page):**

- Include primary keyword
- Clear, benefit-driven
- 60 characters or less

**H2s:**

- Support main topic
- Include secondary keywords naturally
- Organize content logically

**H3s:**

- Break down H2 sections
- Support scannability
- Natural keyword variations

**Meta Description:**

- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword
- Compelling benefit or value proposition
- Call to action when appropriate

**URL Structure:**

- Short, descriptive
- Include primary keyword
- Use hyphens between words
- Lowercase

### Keyword Placement

**Natural Integration:**

- Primary keyword in H1
- Secondary keywords in H2s
- Variations throughout body copy
- Never sacrifice readability for keywords
- Focus on semantic relevance, not exact matches

## Copy Alternatives Strategy

Provide 2-3 versions for key elements:

**When to provide alternatives:**

- Headlines (always)
- Subheadlines (always)
- CTAs (when testing different value props)
- Key benefit statements

**How to differentiate alternatives:**

- Version A: Direct, functional
- Version B: Emotional, aspirational
- Version C: Question-based or story-driven

**Include annotations explaining:**

- The strategic intent behind each version
- Which audience segment each might resonate with
- Pros/cons of each approach

## Formatting Guidelines

**Headers:** Use proper markdown hierarchy (# H1, ## H2, ### H3)

**Body Copy:** Standard paragraphs, 2-4 lines each

**Lists:**

- Bullet points for unordered lists
- Numbered lists for sequential steps or rankings
- Keep list items parallel in structure

**Emphasis:**

- Bold for important terms or key phrases (sparingly)
- Italics for subtle emphasis or quotes
- NEVER use all caps except for acronyms

**White Space:**

- Line break between paragraphs
- Space before and after headers
- Breathing room around CTAs

## Voice & Tone Adaptation

**Professional Services:** Authoritative but approachable
**SaaS:** Modern, efficient, smart
**Healthcare:** Empathetic, trustworthy, clear
**Legal:** Confident, precise, protective
**Financial:** Secure, strategic, growth-oriented
**Creative Services:** Innovative, authentic, bold
**Local Services:** Friendly, reliable, community-focused

Adapt vocabulary, sentence structure, and formality to match industry expectations while maintaining clarity.
